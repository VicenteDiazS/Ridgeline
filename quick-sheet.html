Critical At A Glance

Emergency Card

OEM Tire 245/60R18 105H

Honda feature and accessory documentation list this as the stock size.

Cold Tire Pressure 35 psi front / 35 psi rear

Use the driver door placard as final authority for your exact truck.

Wheel Nut Torque 94 lb-ft

127 N-m. Tighten in a star pattern and re-check after a short drive.

Towing Capacity 2WD 3,500 lb / AWD 5,000 lb

Honda states these are maximum values when properly equipped.

Towing fuel note from Honda: premium unleaded is recommended when towing more than 3,500 lb. Always confirm final limits in your owner's manual and door placard before loading or towing.
